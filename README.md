## Welcome to planet Virentis!!!

The planet of agriculturing, cooking, and trading.

Right here, you will achieve your dream of becoming a farmer in Factorio, it design to be mixture of Factorio + Stardew Valley.

This planet require to complete Gleba first. Easier than Aquilo, but harder than Gleba.

Concept is simple, you plant, you cook, and you trade for unique materials.


## IMPORTANT

This mod is in beta state, which means it will have many bugs and unbalance, feel free to report them and suggest in the discussion tab.

Some items may not much useful, because it's plan to use in further update.

When a major update come, it has a very high chance that you need to regenerate the map.
I suggested you to play this mod again or wait for final version.

## Core features

### Machines

- Oven
- Deep fryer
- Environment research center: virentis lab

### Decoration System

They act like a beacon, small benefit, but need no energy and seperate beacon count from normal beacon, so why not!

While adding aesthetics to the terrain. Now, your factory will look like a real city instead of just factory.

### Reward

- Better nutrients production everywhere
- Living inserter: inserter without electric
